Our 11-Year Story

A Decade of Conversations, Flavors & Warmth

Nestled within the Chandrolok Complex on Bhatchala Road, Gossip was created with a heartfelt philosophy: to provide Bardhaman with a warm, welcoming space where food ignites effortless conversation.

Entering our 11th milestone year, we continue to handcraft every sauce from scratch, source fresh local produce, and fire authentic tandoors daily. Certified with FSSAI License No. 22819029001774, our commitment to food safety and uncompromised taste remains absolute.
