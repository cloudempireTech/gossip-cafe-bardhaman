The Story Behind The Name

Where Good Food Sparks Endless Conversations

In Bengali culture, "Adda" and lively conversation are inseparable from good food. In 2014, Gossip was born inside Chandrolok Complex with a single vision: to create an oasis where friends, families, college youth, and professionals could gather over freshly made comfort food without compromise.

As we step into our 11th year ("দশ পেরিয়ে এগারোয় পা"), Gossip has grown into one of Bardhaman's most cherished dining landmarks. What began as a humble cafe is today a full-scale culinary destination serving artisanal Italian pastas, thin-crust wood-fired pizzas, sizzling charcoal tandoors, Pan-Asian wok classics, and traditional festive platters.

Culinary Values

The Gossip Kitchen Standard

Four golden rules that have defined our kitchen for more than a decade.

🔥

Real Charcoal Tandoor

We never use electric skewers or artificial smoke. Our kebabs and naans are slow-charred over natural glowing lump charcoal for that authentic smoky crust.

🌾

Fresh Dough Hand-Rolled

Our pizza dough and bao buns are prepared fresh daily using fermented yeast cultures, unbleached flour, and pure extra virgin olive oil.

🍲

Zero Pre-Packaged Gravy

Every sauce—from our velvety Alfredo Bechamel to our aromatic Kadhai Masala—is simmered from raw tomatoes, fresh dairy, and hand-ground spices.

❤️

Family Hospitality

We treat every patron as an honored house guest. Our floor team ensures customized spice levels, attentive service, and pure dining joy.
